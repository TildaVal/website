How to Recognise a Legitimate Message from Us

With our new look, you might be concerned that some communications are not genuine. Here's how to stay safe:

We'll never :
  • Direct you to a webpage requesting User IDs, Passwords, OTPs, PINs or secure info
  • Discuss account activity or potential fraud via email
  • Ask you to make payments or transfers to another account without
    verifying your identity or proper authorization.
